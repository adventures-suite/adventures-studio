# AI Travel Advisor

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Vision

**Last Updated:** July 31, 2026

---

# Vision

AdventuresSuite includes an intelligent travel partner that helps users dream, plan, experience, preserve, and publish meaningful adventures.

The AI is not a chatbot.

The AI is a travel companion.

Its purpose is to help users create better adventures and preserve them more beautifully.

---

# Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence should never replace the traveler.

It should amplify the traveler.

The adventure always belongs to the user.

The stories always belong to the user.

AI simply makes it easier to preserve them.

---

# The Five Phases

Every adventure progresses through five phases.

Dream

↓

Plan

↓

Travel

↓

Preserve

↓

Publish

The AI participates in each phase.

---

# Phase 1

## Dream

Help users imagine future adventures.

Examples:

"I'd like to visit Spain."

"I've always wanted to see Japan."

"We're thinking about an Alaska cruise."

The AI should help users:

- discover destinations
- compare itineraries
- estimate budgets
- identify best seasons
- recommend routes

The AI learns travel preferences over time.

---

# Phase 2

## Plan

Planning is collaborative.

The AI helps organize.

The traveler decides.

Planning assistance includes:

Flights

Hotels

Cruises

Tours

Transportation

Packing

Budgeting

Scheduling

Restaurant ideas

Photography opportunities

Weather awareness

Accessibility

Walking distances

Travel documents

The AI should remember user preferences.

---

# Personalized Planning

The AI should gradually learn:

Preferred travel pace

Budget

Luxury level

Cruise preferences

Hotel preferences

Photography interests

History interests

Walking ability

Favorite destinations

Favorite tour styles

Preferred airlines

Favorite restaurants

Previous adventures

Planning becomes increasingly personalized.

---

# Example

Instead of saying:

"You should visit Barcelona."

The AI should say:

"Based on your previous adventures and your interest in history, architecture, photography, and boutique hotels, I think Barcelona and Seville would fit your travel style particularly well."

---

# Phase 3

## Travel

During travel the AI becomes a companion.

Examples:

Today's itinerary

Walking directions

Restaurant suggestions

Packing reminders

Weather changes

Reservation reminders

Port information

Transportation

Historical context

Photography suggestions

Daily journals

Translation assistance

The AI should reduce stress.

Not create more.

---

# Phase 4

## Preserve

After travel the AI becomes a storyteller.

Examples:

Organize photographs

Detect duplicates

Identify landmarks

Create captions

Suggest hero photographs

Generate alternative text

Organize galleries

Build destination pages

Suggest reflections

Create summaries

Draft stories

Preserve the author's voice.

Never replace it.

---

# Phase 5

## Publish

Publishing should become almost automatic.

Outputs include:

Website

Book

EPUB

PDF

Print-ready files

Interactive editions

Everything is generated from the Adventure.

Not:

"It answered my question."
