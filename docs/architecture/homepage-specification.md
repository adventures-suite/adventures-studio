# Homepage Specification

## Purpose

The homepage is the digital front door to **The Simonton Adventures**.

It should feel like opening a premium travel book rather than visiting a conventional website.

The experience should be:

- Cinematic
- Editorial
- Personal
- Timeless
- Photography-led
- Calm and immersive

The homepage should introduce the overall series, establish Steve and Dianne’s voice, present the current volume, and guide visitors into destinations and companion content.

---

## Primary Goals

The homepage should help visitors understand:

1. What The Simonton Adventures is
2. Who Steve and Dianne are
3. Which journey is currently featured
4. Where they can begin exploring
5. How the website connects to the books
6. Why they should continue reading

---

## Experience Principles

### Photography leads

Photography should carry the emotional weight of the homepage.

Text should support the photographs rather than compete with them.

### Minimal interface

The page should avoid feeling like a dashboard, corporate site, or blog template.

Navigation, buttons, and cards should remain visually restrained.

### Editorial pacing

The layout should alternate between:

- large photography
- focused narrative
- generous whitespace
- structured journey content
- personal journal excerpts

### Personal voice

The site should feel unmistakably connected to Steve and Dianne.

The homepage should introduce their shared purpose and include reflections from both of them.

### Mobile first

Every section must remain readable, elegant, and emotionally effective on a phone.

---

# Homepage Structure

## 1. Home Hero

### Purpose

Create an immediate emotional connection and introduce the brand.

### Content

- Full-screen travel photograph
- The Simonton Adventures
- Short brand statement
- Begin the Adventure link
- Minimal navigation

### Suggested copy

**The Simonton Adventures**

Every journey begins with a story.

### Layout

- Minimum height: approximately 90 viewport height
- Full-bleed background image
- Dark cinematic gradient overlay
- Brand text positioned in the lower-left or center-left
- Scroll prompt near the bottom
- Navigation visually secondary to the photograph

### Interaction

- Gentle entrance animation
- Subtle image movement or scale effect
- Animated scroll indicator
- Respect reduced-motion preferences

### Responsive behavior

On smaller screens:

- Maintain a strong photographic crop
- Reduce title size without losing impact
- Keep the primary message above the fold
- Move navigation into a compact menu

---

## 2. Our Story

### Purpose

Explain why Steve and Dianne created The Simonton Adventures.

### Content

- Section label: Our Story
- Short emotional introduction
- One portrait or travel photograph
- Steve and Dianne attribution

### Suggested copy

Travel is not simply about checking places off a list.

It is about slowing down, learning from history, meeting remarkable people, sharing unforgettable moments, and creating memories together.

### Layout

Desktop:

- Two-column editorial layout
- Copy on one side
- Portrait or travel photograph on the other

Mobile:

- Single-column layout
- Text first
- Photograph beneath it

### Tone

Warm, personal, reflective, and concise.

---
