# Adventures Studio Origin

Originally the project began as a companion website for a travel memoir called *The Simonton Adventures.*

As discussions evolved, it became clear the long-term opportunity was much larger.

Rather than becoming only a publishing company, Adventures Studio would become a technology-enabled travel experience company.

Books remain the heart of the brand.

Technology becomes the bridge between inspiration and experience.

The platform will eventually help customers:

- Discover destinations
- Learn
- Plan
- Reserve
- Experience
- Preserve memories

Every future product should support that vision.
