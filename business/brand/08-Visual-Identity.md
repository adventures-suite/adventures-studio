# The Simonton Adventures Visual Identity

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved Direction

**Owner:** Founders
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# Selected Identity

The approved direction for The Simonton Adventures is the **SA Road and
Sunrise** identity.

The mark combines:

- `S` for Simonton
- `A` for Adventures
- A road formed by the lower curve of the `S`
- A sunrise and horizon representing discovery and what comes next
- Editorial serif typography representing storytelling and publishing

The identity belongs to The Simonton Adventures as the flagship Creator. It is
not the logo for Adventures Studio or AdventuresSuite.

---

# Brand Hierarchy

```text
Adventures Studio
    Company and publisher

AdventuresSuite
    Technology platform

The Simonton Adventures
    Creator, public website, books, photography, and products
```

The Simonton Adventures receives primary branding on its website and Creator
products. Adventures Studio may receive publisher attribution in a colophon,
copyright page, packaging, or other appropriate secondary location.

# Color Palette

## Journey Charcoal

- HEX: `#1A2327`
- RGB: `26, 35, 39`
- Use: Primary wordmark, monogram, typography, and dark surfaces

## Legacy Bronze

- HEX: `#9A6E3A`
- RGB: `154, 110, 58`
- Use: The `A`, sunrise, horizon, accents, and premium print treatments

## Warm Ivory

- HEX: `#F4EFE7`
- RGB: `244, 239, 231`
- Use: Light backgrounds, road negative space, and warm editorial surfaces

The dark-background logo uses a brighter production bronze, `#C69452`, when
additional contrast is required.

CMYK and spot-color values must be selected with the book or print supplier from
physical proofs. Screen conversions are not authoritative print specifications.

---

# Typography

The working wordmark uses a classic Baskerville-style serif with generous
uppercase tracking.

Production uses must employ a properly licensed typeface. Print suppliers should
receive outlined vector artwork or an embedded licensed font. The website SVG
uses a safe serif fallback stack until final wordmark outlines are approved.

Do not retype the wordmark in an unrelated font.

The production asset family includes:

# Usage

## Website

- Use the horizontal logo in the desktop header.
- Use the mark when the full wordmark cannot remain legible.
- Use the simplified favicon at browser-icon sizes.
- Preserve the Creator-owned brand configuration and Resource Engine boundary.

## Coffee-Table Books

- Use the stacked or horizontal logo on the title page.
- Use the mark on the spine, back cover, endpapers, or chapter ornaments.
- Allow the photography and book title to lead the front-cover composition.
- Attribute Adventures Studio as publisher where appropriate rather than
  replacing the Creator identity.

## Photography and Prints

- Use the mark on certificates, packaging, edition cards, and promotional
  materials.
- Do not place a prominent watermark over published photography.
- Never imply that purchasing a print transfers copyright or licensing rights.

---

# Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the width of the monogram's
vertical `A` stem. No text, image edge, or competing graphic should enter this
area.

---

# Minimum Size

- Horizontal logo: minimum recommended width `240px` on screen
- Stacked logo: minimum recommended width `160px` on screen
- Mark: minimum recommended width `40px`
- Favicon: designed for `16px`, `32px`, and larger square uses

When reproduction falls below these limits, use the favicon or a simplified
one-color mark.

---

# Prohibited Uses

Do not:

- Stretch, skew, rotate, or rearrange the logo
- Change colors outside approved variants
- Add gradients, shadows, outlines, or photographic texture
- Place the logo over visually busy photography without a controlled field
- Separate the road, sunrise, `S`, or `A` into unrelated decorative elements
- Use the identity as the Adventures Studio or AdventuresSuite logo
- Replace the wordmark typography casually

# Production Status

The SA Road and Sunrise direction is approved for continued development.

The deterministic SVG suite is suitable for website integration and design
prototyping. Before high-volume printing, foil stamping, trademark registration,
or permanent packaging production:

1. Review the custom geometry with a professional identity designer.
2. Convert the final wordmark to outlined vector paths.
3. Complete trademark and conflict review.
4. Approve physical color and finishing proofs.
5. Archive source and final master files with version history.
